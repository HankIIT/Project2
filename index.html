About Me

Hello, my name is Henry. I am a game design major at IIT (Illinois Institute of Technology). I was born in chicago and lived here most of my life, only moving to iowa for a breif period when I was around 6 before returning 2 years later. I have a younger brother and two dogs. I love video games, board/card games, and tabletop role playing games. I also enjoy writing in my off time.

I consider myself a bit of an artist. I like 3D modeling using the free and open source program Blender. 3D modeling isn't my main interest so I am not as experienced in it as a professional might be, but I have many renders that I am proud of. I particularly enjoy the look of 90s-early2000s cg renders, particularly games like Myst and I try to emulate them in my work.

I love animals, particularly dogs. I have two dogs at home, Maggie and Lynx. They are some of my favorite creatures in the entire world. They are both mutts, with Maggie being mostly boxer and Lynx being mostly staffie. Lynx is around 3 or 4 and really energetic. Maggie on the other hand is around 11, she used to be a lot crazier as a puppy, but has mellowed out. I haven't been able to spend as much time with them now that I am in college, but I love coming home to see them.
